About SetSail

Built where the chart is open and the bandwidth is thin.

SetSail grew out of sea practice on the LPG carrier IINO INEOS VESTA — written between ports in China and the United States, and on watches mid-Pacific and mid-Atlantic, over limited shipboard satellite internet.

It sits at a seam most tools miss: navigation craft from the Maritime University of Szczecin, and the habit of shipping working software. A pure programmer rarely feels the bridge; a pure mariner rarely ships a live app. SetSail lives in between — and will keep gaining new functions while sharpening what already works.

Mykhailo Byshliaha Founder · cadet & future officer · IT agency co-founder
