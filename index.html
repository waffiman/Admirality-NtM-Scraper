ABOUT SETSAIL

Built for the Modern Navigation Bridge.

SetSail unifies hydrographic chart corrections, celestial compass verification, synoptic weather routing, and port depth intelligence into a single offline-first maritime platform — designed to reduce bridge workload and elevate situational awareness at sea.

01 • ARCHITECTURE
Offline-First Engine

03 • ERGONOMICS
